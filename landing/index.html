Preserving Nubian Languages
One Voice at a Time

Languages disappear when voices are lost. Andandy Languages is a community-driven effort to preserve endangered Nubian languages through authentic voice recordings.

Our Mission

Many Nubian languages are passed down orally and remain undocumented. When elders pass away, pronunciation, usage, and meaning are often lost forever.

Andandy Languages exists to preserve these voices — recorded directly from native speakers — for education, research, and future generations.
